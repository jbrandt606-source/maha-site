The Sponsored Trip: How Industry Paid for Influence Abroad

Source note: This section is based on FOIA-released emails and attachments linked below. The original PDFs are provided in full for independent verification.

Open: AKA Indonesia trip (PDF) → Open: Aka Indonesia trip 2 (PDF) → Open: Aka DC slides (PDF) →

One of the clearest examples of lobbying crossing into orchestration appears in the paper trail around a trip to Indonesia. This was not a neutral academic exchange. It was a managed advocacy operation with travel and lodging coordinated through industry channels.

What the documents show

The records show coordinated travel itineraries, lodging arrangements, and transfer logistics tied to policy-facing meetings. This is not how independent science is delivered. This is how influence is delivered: comfortably, efficiently, and on someone else’s tab.

Public funding, private delivery

When publicly funded research is transported, scheduled, and positioned by an interested trade group in front of foreign officials during active policy deliberations, the independence narrative collapses. That is not “sharing science.” That is sponsored policy messaging.

Readers are encouraged to open the PDFs above and verify dates, travel coordination, hotel arrangements, and meeting framing directly.
