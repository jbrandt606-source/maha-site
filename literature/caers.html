FDA CAERS Data

Summaries from the FDA CFSAN Adverse Event Reporting System (CAERS) for products often sold in gas stations and vape shops. Parent-friendly, signal-focused.

Last updated: October 21, 2025
Important context: CAERS is a spontaneous reporting system for foods and dietary supplements. Reports may be incomplete or duplicative and do not prove causation. They do help detect safety signals that deserve attention.

How to read CAERS numbers:
  • Counts reflect what consumers/clinicians reported; one report can list multiple products and reactions.
  • Under-reporting is common; media attention can drive spikes in reports.
  • CAERS covers foods & dietary supplements (different scope than FAERS for drugs/devices).
