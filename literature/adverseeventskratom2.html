FDA Alerts — Kratom (Mitragynine)

This page lists FDA-published alerts only—including warning letters, consumer updates, enforcement actions, and recalls related to kratom (mitragynine) and 7-hydroxymitragynine. No FAERS or CAERS summaries are included here.

Last updated: October 2025
FDA Alerts & Warning Letters — Kratom / 7-OH (Sorted by Date)

Below are **FDA kratom-related actions** (warning letters, recalls, advisories) sorted from **newest to oldest**. Each entry links to the PDF you placed in /literature/fda_alerts/. If any link 404s, verify the filename in your repo and update the href accordingly.

2025

  • Vanguard Enterprises, LLC — Recall (Oct 30, 2025)
    FDA-announced recall of **Monarch Premium Kratom** powders (Bali Gold, Red Bali, Green Maeng Da, White Elephant) for **Salmonella** contamination; distributed online/in stores nationwide (except five states). Download PDF
  • FDA Press Release — “FDA Takes Steps to Restrict 7-OH Opioid Products” (Jul 29, 2025)
    FDA announced recommending **scheduling of 7-hydroxymitragynine** (7-OH) under the CSA; cites potent opioid activity and public-health risk, especially with **child-appealing** products (e.g., gummies). Download PDF
  • Thang Botanicals, Inc. (7ΩHMZ / 7-OHMZ) — Warning Letter (Jun 25, 2025)
    FDA cited 7-OH gummies and kratom shots marketed as foods/supplements. Determined **7-OH and kratom extracts are unsafe food additives** and not GRAS; flagged **addiction, liver/heart toxicity, seizures, respiratory depression**, and child-appealing packaging. Download PDF
  • Hydroxie, LLC — Warning Letter (Jun 25, 2025)
    Cited for marketing **7-OH** tablets, sublingual strips, and drink mixes as **unapproved new drugs** with pain/anxiety/depression/fibromyalgia claims; drinks deemed **adulterated foods** because 7-OH is not an approved/safe additive. Download PDF
  • 7Tabz Retail, LLC — Warning Letter (Jun 25, 2025)
    FDA cited sales of **7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH)** tablets and pseudoephedrine blends as unapproved drugs with pain/mood claims; noted **mu-opioid activity**, adverse event reports, and **child-appealing packaging**; products also deemed adulterated under FD&C Act. Download PDF

2024

  • FDA Consumer Alert — OPMS Black Liquid Kratom (Jul 26, 2024)
    FDA warned consumers not to use **OPMS Black Liquid Kratom** due to multiple serious adverse event reports (including a death). Emphasized that **no kratom product is FDA-approved** and highlighted risks from **mitragynine and 7-OH**. Download PDF
  • FDA & CDC — “FDA and Kratom” (Public Health Overview, 2024)
    FDA reiterates that **kratom is not lawfully marketed** as a drug, food, or dietary supplement; flags risks of **addiction, liver injury, seizures**, neonatal withdrawal, and **heavy-metal/Salmonella** contamination. Notes ongoing FDA research and monitoring. Download PDF

2023

  • Shot of Joy LLC — Warning Letter (Sept 25, 2023)
    FDA warned the firm for marketing kratom/kava **shots and gummies** with **unapproved drug claims** (opioid addiction, depression, anxiety). FDA: products are **unapproved new drugs** and may deter patients from proven therapies. Download PDF

2022–2021

  • Omni Consumer Products LLC (YoKratom) — Warning Letter (Jun 30, 2022)
    FDA/FTC cited YoKratom for advertising kratom strains (Red Maeng Da, Red Bali, White Borneo, etc.) as **treatments for addiction and pain**. Warned of potential **$46,517 per-violation** penalties under the Opioid Addiction Recovery Fraud Prevention Act. Download PDF
  • Herbsens Botanicals — Warning Letter (Jun 30, 2022)
    FDA/FTC warned for marketing kratom as treatments for **pain, depression, diabetes, PTSD, and opioid withdrawal**; described as “morphine-like.” Products deemed **unapproved/misbranded drugs** sold across state lines. Download PDF
  • Umbrella — Warning Letter (May 18, 2021)
    FDA cited sale of unapproved/misbranded products including **kratom powders for opioid withdrawal**, **tianeptine**, **SARMs**, and **COVID-19 claims** for NACET powder across multiple sites — violations of several federal drug laws. Download PDF

2019

2019 FDA Actions — Kratom / 7-OH
  • FDA Alerts Consumers Not to Use Kratom NC Products (Jun 25, 2019)
    FDA reported **microbial contamination** (Klebsiella, Enterobacter, E. coli) in “Kratom NC” products; firm declined to initiate a recall. Concurrent warning letter cited **illegal opioid-treatment claims** and misbranding. Download PDF
  • Public Notification — Detox Plus (Dec 4, 2019)
    FDA testing found **tadalafil** (Rx drug) and kratom alkaloids **paynantheine** and **mitragynine** in “Detox Plus,” sold for “digestive health.” Undeclared drug ingredients pose risks of **hypotension**, drug interactions, and **addiction/abuse**. FDA advised consumers **not to use** the product. Download PDF
  • Cali Botanicals, LLC — Warning Letter (Jun 11, 2019)
    Kratom powders, capsules, and tinctures marketed for **opioid withdrawal, pain, depression, anxiety, and diabetes**. FDA deemed products **unapproved/misbranded drugs**; highlighted claims that kratom “acts as a μ-opioid receptor like morphine” and could delay evidence-based care. Download PDF

2018 FDA Actions — Kratom / 7-OH

2018: Recalls, Seizures & Warning Letters
  • Triangle Pharmanaturals — Mandatory Recall (Apr 17, 2018)
    FDA’s first **mandatory recall** under FSMA after multiple **Salmonella**-positive kratom samples; brands included Raw Form Organics, Molecule, Chameleon, Naturally, Lifted, Live Well, and Third Eye. Distributed across 30+ cities. Download PDF
  • Maya Distribution, LLC — Recall (Apr 30, 2018)
    Recall of **Dragon Kratom** (capsules & powders) after FDA testing detected **Salmonella**; distributed in ~10 states; one illness reported. Company halted production and cooperated with FDA on destruction of product. Download PDF
  • Revibe, Inc. — Warning Letter (May 18, 2018)
    FDA found multiple kratom strains marketed as cures for **pain, arthritis, cancer, stroke, depression, diabetes, hypertension**, and **opioid withdrawal** (“It cures chronic pain”; “eliminate opiate withdrawal”). Products deemed **unapproved/misbranded drugs**. Download PDF
  • Pious Lion — Voluntary Recall (May 23, 2018)
    Voluntary recall of **Pink Bali** & **White Maeng Da** kratom for **Salmonella** contamination (NC/TN distribution). No illnesses reported; FDA advised consumers to stop use and sanitize surfaces. Download PDF
  • Mitra Distributing, Inc. — Warning Letter (Sept 4, 2018)
    Products marketed for **opioid withdrawal, depression, obesity, hypertension, anxiety**; promoted as a “natural alternative” for heroin addiction. Determined to be **unapproved/misbranded drugs** posing public-health risks. Download PDF
2018: Additional FDA Actions
  • Jack B Goods Outlet Store — Warning Letter (Nov 7, 2018)
    “Tianaa Red/White/Green” kratom products deemed **unapproved/misbranded drugs**, illegally marketed as opioid alternatives and anxiety relievers; contained **tianeptine** (not a lawful dietary ingredient). Download PDF
  • Avalon Packaging — Warning Letter (Nov 8, 2018)
    FDA detected **Salmonella** in multiple raw kratom samples at the Utah facility; several strains linked genetically to a multistate outbreak. Serious GMP deficiencies and lack of safety specifications noted; kratom deemed an unapproved new dietary ingredient. Download PDF
  • FDA In Brief — Industrial Chemicals LLC “Mitrasafe” (Feb 26, 2018)
    FDA objected to marketing of **Mitrasafe (≈99% mitragynine)** as a “natural substitute for opium”; toxicology signaled adverse effects at all tested doses. Classified as an **unapproved drug** and **adulterated dietary supplement**. Download PDF
  • Triangle Pharmanaturals — FDA Orders Mandatory Recall (Apr 13, 2018)
    Separate FDA announcement issuing the **mandatory recall order** for Triangle Pharmanaturals after refusal to conduct a voluntary recall; multiple **Salmonella-positive** kratom products (Raw Form Organics, Molecule, Naturally, Lifted, Live Well, Third Eye). Download PDF
  • PDX Aromatics — Expanded Recalls (Mar 2018)
    PDX (Kraken Kratom, Phytoextractum, Soul Speciosa) issued multiple recalls beginning March 2018 after FDA/CDC linked its products to a **multistate Salmonella outbreak**; recalls expanded to additional lots/strains sold in the U.S. and abroad. Download PDF
2019–2016: Additional Actions & Seizures
  • U.S. Marshals Seize Kratom Products — FDA Enforcement (Jan 6, 2016)
    At FDA’s request, U.S. Marshals seized ~**90,000 bottles** of kratom-containing products (RelaKzpro; Dordoniz Natural Products, IL). FDA cited kratom as an **unsafe, adulterated ingredient** with risks including **respiratory depression, withdrawal, vomiting, and organ toxicity**. Download PDF

These FDA actions concern **kratom and 7-OH** in **drug** or **food/dietary** products (e.g., “shots,” gummies, powders, capsules). They focus on **unapproved drug claims**, **adulteration** (e.g., 7-OH in foods/supplements, **Salmonella** contamination), and **misbranding**, as well as **first-of-its-kind mandatory recalls**.
