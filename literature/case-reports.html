Kratom Case Reports

Published peer-reviewed case reports describing real clinical presentations linked to Mitragyna speciosa (kratom). Each summary includes a direct link to the PDF stored in this site’s main repository. For education only — not medical advice.

Liver injury

Kratom: A “Dangerous Player” in the Opioid Crisis

32-year-old man with acute jaundice and cholestatic hepatitis after heavy kratom use. Viral, autoimmune, and metabolic causes excluded. Liver enzymes improved after discontinuation. Authors highlight kratom’s potential hepatotoxicity and misuse amid the opioid crisis.

Neurology

Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy After Kratom + Stimulant Use

22-year-old male developed severe headache and confusion after kratom and amphetamine ingestion. MRI revealed bilateral occipital and cerebellar vasogenic edema (PRES). Improved with supportive care. Suggests synergistic hypertensive effects between kratom and stimulants.

Seizures

Recurrent Seizures from Chronic Kratom Use

19-year-old chronic kratom user presented with recurrent tonic-clonic seizures and MRI changes in the basal ganglia. Seizures ceased after kratom cessation. Authors describe atypical neuro-toxicity from prolonged exposure.

Liver pathology

Histologic Characterization of Kratom-Related Liver Injury

38-year-old man with dark urine and pruritus after kratom ingestion. Biopsy showed portal inflammation with eosinophils, bile duct damage, and canalicular cholestasis. First report detailing histopathology of kratom-induced liver injury.

Endocrine

Kratom-Induced Hyperprolactinemia & Secondary Hypogonadism

42-year-old male presented with fatigue and low testosterone. Found to have elevated prolactin while using kratom daily; values normalized after cessation. Highlights opioid-like endocrine suppression via dopamine inhibition.

Addiction

Kratom Addiction and Withdrawal

37-year-old woman developed dependence on kratom extract with severe opioid-type withdrawal: myalgias, chills, anxiety, and GI upset. Managed successfully with clonidine and hydroxyzine. Demonstrates clear addictive potential and cross-tolerance with opioids.

Neonatal

Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome from Maternal Kratom Use

Term infant developed withdrawal (Finnegan >10) despite negative opioid tox screen. Mother reported chronic kratom use for pain. Infant required oral morphine taper. Authors stress including kratom exposure in NAS differential diagnosis.

Maternal & Neonatal

Maternal and Neonatal Kratom Dependence & Withdrawal

Postpartum mother using large daily kratom doses developed dependency; her newborn required NICU morphine for withdrawal symptoms. Demonstrates transplacental exposure and dependence potential.

Cardiac / Toxicity

Death from Kratom Toxicity and the Possible Role of Intralipid

26-year-old male suffered cardiac arrest ~24 h after kratom ingestion. Temporary response to IV lipid emulsion (“lipid rescue”) before multi-organ failure. Authors propose cardiotoxic metabolites and discuss emergency treatment options.
