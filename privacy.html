Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2025

Mothers Against Herbal Abuse (“MAHA,” “we,” “us”) operates an educational website to help families understand risks from unregulated “herbal” and gas-station products. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices. If you have questions, email contact@mothersagainstherbalabuse.org.

Information We Collect

  • Basic site logs. Like most sites, our host may record IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and performance.
  • Analytics. We use privacy-respecting analytics (e.g., GA4) to understand page views and improve content. Data is aggregated; we do not build personal profiles.
  • Voluntary messages. If you email us or submit a story, we receive the information you provide (e.g., name, email, message).

How We Use Information

  • Operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • Respond to questions and support advocacy/education efforts.
  • Publish de-identified statistics (never your personal story without explicit consent).

Cookies & Analytics

We may use first-party cookies or analytics to measure site usage (e.g., which pages are most helpful). You can use your browser’s settings to clear or block cookies. Analytics data is used in aggregate form.

Child & Teen Safety

Our content is designed for parents and community leaders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with service providers (e.g., site hosting or analytics) for the sole purpose of running the site. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of analytics via your browser or extensions.
  • Do not email sensitive personal or medical details; if you do, ask us to delete them.
  • Request deletion of messages you previously sent: contact@mothersagainstherbalabuse.org.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures. For responsible security disclosures, see /.well-known/security.txt.

Third-Party Links

We link to government/health resources. Those sites have their own policies; review them before sharing information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this page to reflect changes in our practices. The “Last updated” date shows when the policy was revised.
