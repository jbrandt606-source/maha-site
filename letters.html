Write a Letter to Your Lawmaker

Your voice matters. A short, personal letter from a parent or community member is one of the most effective ways to move policy. This guide shows you what to say, how to say it, and gives you a copy-ready template you can send today.

How to Write an Effective Letter

1) Know your goal. Do you want the lawmaker to support a bill, introduce one, or ask the Health Department for a warning? Your goal shapes the tone and the ask.

2) Use a simple structure. Greeting → Who you are → What’s happening → Why it matters → What you want → Thanks + contact info.

3) Make it personal. One short story is more memorable than five statistics. Add one sentence about how local families are affected.

4) Be specific in the ask. “Please support legislation to restrict or ban retail sales of unapproved psychoactive products such as kratom and tianeptine” or “Please request a DOH advisory.”

5) Keep it short. One page. Clear. Respectful. Thank them for their time and offer to share resources at MothersAgainstHerbalAbuse.org.

Tips for Impact

One page wins. Clear, respectful, and specific beats long and technical.

Facts + emotion. A single data point plus a short story is memorable and persuasive.

Follow up. Send a thank-you, ask for a meeting, or attend office hours. Staff are your allies.

Send to multiple targets. City/County Council, State Reps/Senators, and your U.S. House/Senators.
