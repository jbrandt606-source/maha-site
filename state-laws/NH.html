New Hampshire (NH) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: No specific scheduling located (as of 2025).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: No statewide ban/regulation noted in 50-state summaries.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Addressed via general controlled-drug/inhalant framework; check local rules.
  • THC / Cannabis: Medical program only; no adult-use retail.
  • Salvia: Not scheduled statewide.
  • Amanita muscaria: Generally lawful/not scheduled.
  • “Mad honey”: No NH-specific ban; CDC medical risk framing applies.
Parent tip: Keep any “boosters” or “relaxers” away from kids; report adverse effects to your health department.
