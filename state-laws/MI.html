Michigan (MI) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Schedule II (since 2018). PA 107 of 2018 added tianeptine sodium to Schedule II.
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: Legal statewide; watch local actions / future bills.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Recreational inhalation prohibited under general inhalant law; local ordinances exist.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal; active, evolving regulatory framework.
  • Salvia / Amanita: No specific statewide bans located.
  • “Mad honey”: No MI-specific ban; use food-safety risk framing.
Parent tip: “Natural” labels can still mean potent drugs. Store edibles and capsules out of kids’ reach.
