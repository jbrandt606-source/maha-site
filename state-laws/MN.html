Minnesota (MN) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Listed as Schedule I by state reference compendia (verify via Board / 152.02 updates).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: 18+ only; minor-sale/possession offenses codified (2018 law, Minn. Stat. § 152.027, subd. 7).
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Regulated in healthcare contexts; misuse covered by general laws.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal (2023); OCM implementing retail.
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No MN-specific ban; food hazard framing applies.
Parent tip: Bright, candy-style labeling is a risk signal — keep those items out of sight and reach.
