Rhode Island (RI) — What’s Legal

Checked Jan 2025 (RIDOH list) & Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Not listed by name (as of Jan 2025 RIDOH sheet).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: Schedule I statewide (RIDOH list: “mitragynine or hydroxymitragynine”). 2025 bills propose a Kratom Act (21+, labeling) but do not de-schedule.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Covered under inhalant statutes; local enforcement likely.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal (2022 Cannabis Act).
  • Salvia: Schedule I (ban enacted 2014).
  • Amanita muscaria: Not scheduled in RI.
  • “Mad honey”: No RI-specific ban; use medical risk framing (bradycardia, hypotension).
Parent tip: “Herbal relaxers” may be illegal in RI—check labels for kratom or salvia before purchase.
