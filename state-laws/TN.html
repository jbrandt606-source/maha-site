Tennessee (TN) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Schedule II since July 1, 2022.
  • Phenibut: Not specifically scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: Legal with restrictions on synthetics/altered alkaloids; natural botanical allowed.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Unlawful to sell/possess/use for intoxication under state inhalant statute.
  • THC / Cannabis: No full medical program; limited low-THC/CBD only.
  • Salvia divinorum: Prohibited (salvinorin A/salvia offenses codified).
  • Amanita muscaria: Generally legal/not scheduled.
  • “Mad honey”: No TN-specific ban; food safety/adulteration framework applies.
Parent tip: Report tianeptine (“gas-station heroin”) and avoid “enhanced” kratom or unlabeled “relax/focus” shots.
