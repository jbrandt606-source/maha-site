Wyoming (WY) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Not scheduled (as of 2025).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA position applies.
  • Kratom: Legal; no statewide KCPA yet (monitor local retail rules).
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Addressed under general inhalant misuse statutes.
  • THC / Cannabis: No medical or adult-use program (hemp CBD only).
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No WY-specific ban; food hazard framing.
Parent tip: “Legal herbal” products can hide synthetic drugs—avoid items without verified testing labels.
