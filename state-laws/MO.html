Missouri (MO) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Bill to add to Schedule I moving (HB 2213); verify final status.
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: Regulated (Kratom Consumer Protection Act) — age limits + labeling; 2025 tightening debated.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: No statewide N₂O-specific ban; patchwork local rules; MOST brief notes no MO-wide restriction on N₂O itself.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal (Amendment 3, 2022); program active; tax issues clarified by MO Supreme Court.
  • Salvia divinorum / Salvinorin A: Schedule I in MO.
  • Amanita muscaria: Not scheduled.
  • “Mad honey”: No MO-specific ban; food law + FDA/CDC toxicology applies.
Parent tip: “Legal” doesn’t equal “kid-safe.” Keep candy-look edibles and capsules locked away.
