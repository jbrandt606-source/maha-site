Nebraska (NE) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: No statewide schedule (as of 2025). (LB 475 to schedule was folded into other work, not enacted.)
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: KCPA enacted 2025 (LB 230) — registration, labeling, 21+, adulterant limits.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: General laws apply; no NE-specific N₂O statute singled out.
  • THC / Cannabis: Medical program approved by voters (2024); rulemaking underway (details evolving).
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No NE-specific ban; treat as food hazard.
Parent tip: Check labels for “leaf,” “kanna,” or stimulant stacks; ask for COAs and age checks.
