Oregon (OR) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: No Oregon-specific schedule found (as of 2025).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA misbranding position applies.
  • Kratom: Regulated statewide (21+; labeling and registration required under 2022 Oregon Laws ch. 41).
  • Nitrous oxide: No statewide ban; misuse covered under general laws; professional/medical use regulated.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal; regulated by OCM (former OLCC).
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No OR-specific ban; use food-safety framing.
Parent tip: “Herbal relax” shots and capsules often mix unlisted stimulants—avoid unlabeled imports.
