Alaska (AK) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • This is a parent-friendly summary, not legal advice.

  • Tianeptine: Not specifically scheduled statewide (N/A on 50-state summaries).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled. (Still not a lawful dietary ingredient per FDA.)
  • Kratom: Legal statewide; no KCPA. Watch for local actions.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Inhalant misuse prohibited under state law; municipalities have moved to bar sales to minors (e.g., N₂O, poppers, kratom).
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal (Measure 2, 2014; sales since 2015).
  • Salvia divinorum: Not scheduled statewide.
  • Amanita muscaria (muscimol): Not scheduled.
  • “Mad honey” (grayanotoxins): No AK-specific ban; treat as food hazard (risk of bradycardia, hypotension, neuro symptoms).
Parent tip: “Herbal” on a label does not mean safe or legal. Ask retailers for age checks and remove candy-style packages from kids’ reach.
