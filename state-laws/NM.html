New Mexico (NM) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Not scheduled (as of 2025).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA position applies.
  • Kratom: Legal (no statewide KCPA yet); retail exists — watch for 21+ and labeling proposals.
  • Nitrous oxide: General inhalant provisions apply; local enforcement possible.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal statewide; regulated retail in place.
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No NM-specific ban; food hazard framing.
Parent tip: Products sold as “herbal mood boosters” can include DEA-flagged chemicals — check ingredients and report adverse effects.
