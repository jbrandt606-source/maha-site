Wisconsin (WI) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: No statewide schedule found (as of 2025); track bills if filed.
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: Banned statewide — mitragynine & 7-OH listed as Schedule I; removal petition discussed in 2024 CSB materials.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Inhalant misuse criminalized; prosecutions occur.
  • THC / Cannabis: No medical/adult-use program (proposals ongoing).
  • Salvia / Amanita: No separate amanita listing; salvia not uniquely scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No WI-specific ban; treat as food hazard.
Parent tip: “Boosters” next to snacks ≠ safe. Check labels for DMHA/PEA/kanna/caffeine stacks.
