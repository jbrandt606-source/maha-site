Vermont (VT) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: No statewide scheduling found (as of 2025).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: No statewide ban noted; watch 2025–26 filings.
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: Addressed mainly via general consumer-product rules; local ordinances may apply.
  • THC / Cannabis: Adult-use legal; 2025 Act 56 (H.321) delivered technical updates to regulation.
  • Salvia: Not scheduled statewide.
  • Amanita muscaria: Generally lawful/not scheduled.
  • “Mad honey”: No VT-specific ban; food-safety risk language applies.
Parent tip: “Ancient” or “adaptogen” branding doesn’t guarantee safety—avoid multi-stack capsules without lab tests.
