North Dakota (ND) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13, 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: No statewide schedule (as of 2025).
  • Phenibut: Not scheduled; FDA: not a lawful dietary ingredient.
  • Kratom: No statewide ban; 2025 bills pivoted toward study/possible regulation (age/registration ideas surfaced).
  • Nitrous oxide / inhalants: General inhalant rules; no specific N₂O ban noted.
  • THC / Cannabis: Medical program active (ND HHS).
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No ND-specific ban; use food-safety framing.
Parent tip: “Herbal highs” at checkout are not harmless — treat unlabeled shots like drugs and keep away from kids.
