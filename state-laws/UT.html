Utah (UT) — What’s Legal

Checked Oct 13 2025 • Parent-friendly summary (not legal advice).

  • Tianeptine: Schedule I (added by HB 173 in 2025).
  • Phenibut: Schedule I (HB 173 2025 also added phenibut).
  • Kratom: Regulated since 2019 (Kratom Consumer Protection Act, Title 4 Ch. 45); processor registration, labeling, no sales to <18.
  • Nitrous oxide: Covered under general inhalant statutes; medical/dental nitrous separately regulated.
  • THC / Cannabis: Medical cannabis legal; adult-use not legal.
  • Salvia / Amanita: Not scheduled statewide.
  • “Mad honey”: No UT-specific ban; food hazard framing.
Parent tip: Even legal kratom products should list processor IDs and lab tests; avoid “enhanced 7-OH” versions.
