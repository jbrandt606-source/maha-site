About MAHA • Mothers Against Herbal Abuse

Created by a pharmacist and mother outraged by the sight of kratom and tianeptine sold beside candy and energy drinks, MAHA is a grassroots coalition of parents, clinicians, and advocates dedicated to exposing how kratom’s active alkaloid 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) and other “herbal” drugs have infiltrated U.S. convenience stores under weak supplement laws.

“Are we supposed to make America healthy again — or make America high again?”
For us, the answer is clear: we choose health, knowledge, and community.

About MAHA

MAHA began when its founder, a licensed pharmacist and mother, discovered that gas stations and vape shops were openly selling addictive “herbal” products—powders, capsules, and drinks containing unapproved drugs marketed as natural supplements.

Today MAHA stands as a non-partisan educational initiative dedicated to exposing the full spectrum of unregulated plant-derived and synthetic substances sold under the “herbal wellness” label. From kratom and kava to phenibut, tianeptine, and delta-8 THC, we highlight how these products exploit supplement loopholes, create dependence, and harm families.

Our mission is simple: arm parents, educators, and policymakers with clear science, transparent data, and the tools to protect communities from addiction disguised as alternative health.

Our Focus Areas

Scientific Clarity. Explaining how these "herbal" supplements lead to addiction.

Accountability. Tracking how lobbyists influence state legislation and public perception.

Community Education. Providing parents and healthcare workers with evidence-based summaries and plain-language guides.

Advocacy Tools. Templates and resources to contact lawmakers, local councils, and health agencies demanding reform.

Our Mission

  • Educate families about kratom, tianeptine, phenibut, and other unregulated drugs misrepresented as “herbal.”
  • Expose misleading narratives promoted by lobbying groups that downplay risk and target teens.
  • Empower parents and professionals to push for stronger state and federal action.

When facts meet compassion, change follows. MAHA works to make America healthy again — not high again.
